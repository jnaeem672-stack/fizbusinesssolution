## Key finding (honest)
GSC does NOT hold a "high-impression, low-CTR BUYER query" for any of these 3 pages.
The impressions these pages get are either (a) Arabic "meaning of assignment" informational queries,
or (b) university-name queries — neither is a UK commercial-intent buyer query.

Therefore target keywords are chosen from **buyer intent + keyword research (Semrush volume)**,
not from GSC impressions. The pages are kept on non-overlapping targets:
- assignment-help-uk -> "university assignment help UK" (flagship, broad UK buyer intent)
- mba-assignment-help -> MBA vertical ("MBA assignment / MBA dissertation help")
- assignment-help-saudi-arabia -> Saudi geo (English page serving KSA)
